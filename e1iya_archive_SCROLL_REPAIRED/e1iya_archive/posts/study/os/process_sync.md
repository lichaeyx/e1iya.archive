# Process Synchronization

## Critical Section

Shared data access needs a consistent synchronization strategy.

## Mutex and Semaphore

A mutex controls exclusive ownership. A semaphore maintains a count of available permits.

## Observation

Correctness depends on both safety and progress properties.
